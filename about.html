About

A small studio that ships like a big one.

Saebaan is a two-person product studio. We design, engineer, and grow web applications and the brands around them — and we stay hands-on from first sketch to production.

We started Saebaan because the best work happens when the people talking to you are the same people building the thing. No hand-offs to junior teams, no account managers translating your idea into a brief into a ticket until the intent is gone.

So we keep it small on purpose. Fewer people, more ownership, and a direct line to whoever is making the product you paid for. We take on a handful of projects at a time and give each one real attention.

Between us we cover the whole stack — product design and brand, full-stack engineering, and the infrastructure and automation that keeps it running. That means one team can take an idea from a blank page to something live and looked-after.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Web applications, dashboards and internal tools, brand and identity work, and the app development, AI automation and DevOps around them. If it's a digital product for an ambitious team, it's probably a fit.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, but most engagements start in the low five figures. Tell us what you're building on the contact page and we'll come back with a realistic range.

How long does it take?

A focused MVP is usually a few weeks; a full product with brand and infrastructure is a few months. We ship in slices so you see progress the whole way.

Who owns the work?

You do. Code, designs, and assets are yours. We can hand everything over or stay on to run and improve it — your call.

Do you work with teams outside the UK?

Yes. We're UK-based but work with teams remotely wherever they are.
